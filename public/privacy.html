Didaskalia privacy policy

Last updated: 2026.

Didaskalia is a Bible quiz app. This page explains what we collect and why. In short: your name, email and points, so that you can sign in and compete, and nothing else.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (the password is stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider, Supabase) and the display name you choose.
  • Game data: your points, streaks, scores, badges, saved questions and verse-memory progress, so that your progress follows you to another phone.
  • Usage counts: that the app was opened on a device (a random ID, not your name or your phone’s hardware ID), the type of device (Android app, installed web app or website), when you were last active, and how many rounds were played. We use these counts to understand how many people use the app.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, advertising ID or payment details. We show no ads and do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

Who can see what

  • Other players can see your display name and points on the leaderboard and in groups you join. You can hide yourself from the public leaderboard in Settings.
  • Your email address is never shown to other players.
  • The app’s administrators can see counts, and the display names and join dates of members.

Where it is stored

Account and game data is stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, which keeps it secure and encrypted in transit. Some progress is also saved on your device.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Menu → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, points and saved progress. If you cannot open the app, email us from the address you signed up with and we will delete it. See how to delete your account.

Children

Didaskalia is meant for general audiences. If you are a parent and believe your child has created an account without your permission, contact us and we will delete it.
