Delete your Didaskalia account

In the app

  1. Open Didaskalia and sign in.
  2. Tap the three-line menu, then Settings.
  3. Scroll to Delete account, tap Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm.

Your account, leaderboard points, group scores and saved progress are removed immediately and permanently.

Can’t open the app?

Email support@excellenteaser.com from the address you registered with, and we will delete your account within 7 days.
